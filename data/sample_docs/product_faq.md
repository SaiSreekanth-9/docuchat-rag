# PulseBoard — Product FAQ

## What is PulseBoard?
PulseBoard is a real-time analytics dashboard by Nimbus Analytics. It connects to your
product database or warehouse and renders live KPI dashboards without writing SQL.

## Pricing tiers
- **Starter — $29/month**: 3 dashboards, 1 data source, 7-day data history, community support.
- **Pro — $99/month**: unlimited dashboards, 10 data sources, 13-month data history,
  anomaly alerts, Slack integration, priority email support.
- **Enterprise — custom pricing**: SSO/SAML, dedicated CSM, VPC deployment, custom
  data-retention terms, 99.99% SLA.

## Support hours
- Starter and Pro: email support, Monday–Friday, 9 AM–6 PM Central Time.
- Enterprise: 24/7 phone + email support with a 1-hour response SLA.

## Data retention
- Starter workspaces retain data for 7 days; Pro for 13 months.
- After cancellation, customer data is retained for **30 days** and then permanently
  deleted. Enterprise customers can negotiate custom retention terms.

## Integrations
Native integrations: PostgreSQL, Snowflake, BigQuery, Stripe, Segment, Slack.
A REST API and webhook support are available on Pro and Enterprise.

## Security & compliance
PulseBoard is SOC 2 Type II certified. Data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at
rest (AES-256). EU customer data is stored in the Frankfurt region.
