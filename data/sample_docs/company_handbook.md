# Nimbus Analytics — Employee Handbook (excerpts)

## About the company
Nimbus Analytics is a 240-person SaaS company headquartered in Austin, Texas, founded in
2019. We build PulseBoard, a real-time analytics dashboard for product teams.

## Working hours & time off
- Standard working hours are 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM Central Time, Monday to Friday.
- Full-time employees receive **20 vacation days per year**, accrued monthly. Unused days
  (up to 5) roll over to the next year.
- There are 10 company holidays per year, plus 5 paid sick days.

## Remote work policy
- Nimbus Analytics is **remote-first**. Employees may work from anywhere in the United States.
- Engineers are expected to be online and reachable during core collaboration hours,
  10:00 AM to 3:00 PM Central Time.
- Home-office stipend: $1,200 one-time plus $75/month for internet and co-working.

## Security policy
- All employees must enable multi-factor authentication (MFA) on every work account.
- If you suspect a **phishing email**, do NOT click any links. Forward it to
  security@nimbus.example and delete it from your inbox. The security team responds
  within 4 business hours.
- Laptops must have full-disk encryption enabled; contact IT if yours does not.

## Expense policy
- Expenses under $200 need no pre-approval; anything above requires manager approval
  in Ramp before purchase.
- Travel bookings go through the Navan portal. Per-diem for meals is $75/day.
